# FM27 Steam build 26227640 (Launch Preview)

Observed on 2026-10-31 from the Steam install under
`D:\SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\Football Manager 27\` (app 4530370, build
26227640), with a test save on 13 July 2026: 38,007 players, 21,510 staff and 23,606
clubs. The save is a smaller database than the one on Game Pass, not another game.

## The binary

`game_plugin.dll` as mapped in memory:

| | Steam | Game Pass 0.9.2355.0 |
|---|---|---|
| Size of image | `0x19E69000` | `0x19436000` |
| Link timestamp | `0x6ADFB11C` | `0x6ADFB161` |

The two were linked 69 seconds apart, from the same source. They are packaged
differently: Steam's has its own protected sections (a large `.code` section that is
read-write-execute, `.srdata`, `.arch` and others).

## Same layout, other addresses

- **Entity arrays:** `calibrate` found them by scanning, with no profile, in 2.2 s,
  at the RVAs fm-editor noted on Steam:
  - players at `0x57037D0`;
  - staff at `0x5703818`;
  - clubs at `0x5703988`.

  Every sampled element decoded. `profiles/fm27/steam-26227640.toml` now names
  these, so the app connects without scanning.
- **Object layout:** `profiles/fm27/layout.toml` holds for Steam unchanged. The same
  values were read as on Game Pass, or as fm-editor saw them:
  - Lionel Messi: 207 caps and 125 goals, 18 and 14 for the U21s, his attributes.
  - Tottenham Hotspur:
    - founded 1882, 50,000 season tickets, 6.8M social followers;
    - supporter traits 18/14/7/14/7/14;
    - a £65 ticket, corporate facilities 20, reputation 7650;
    - its full finances.
  - Manchester United:
    - Bruno Fernandes captain, Lisandro Martínez vice-captain;
    - its senior, under-21 and under-18 lists, with injuries, loans, energy,
      sharpness and morale.
  - Tottenham's captains: Micky van de Ven and Pedro Porro.
- **Store and version:** the app tells the store from the path (`\steamapps\`). It
  shows Steam's build id, read from the `appmanifest_*.acf` that names the install
  folder.
- **The byte at `+0x1F1`:** it reads 0 for every Manchester United player here. This
  is one more sign that it is not the happiness the game shows (see the Game Pass
  notes); morale at `+0x26C` reads 5 to 18.

- **`move`:** Mohammed Kudus to Juventus and back, in 11 writes.
- **`swap`:** Kudus and Weston McKennie and back, in 14 writes.

  - Amad as captain;

The human manager runs Arsenal in this save, so Arsenal's players are the ones a move
refuses.

## The app

The release build was run against this save, driven over CDP, with every change
undone:
- **Connecting:** with the build profile. The connection line reads "FM27 · Steam build
  26227640" ("derleme" in Turkish).
- **Edits:** Messi's and Mikel Arteta's fields, Messi's contract and Tottenham's club
  values, by command and through the profiles.
- **Mass edits:** 222 changes on Tottenham's 111 players, and 111 through the dialog,
  each undone as one batch.
- **A move:** through the profile's dialog. A player of the human club, the same club
  and a past contract end were refused.
- **Injuries and live values:**
  - healing, and its undo;
  - energy, and match sharpness for Tottenham's 80 players;
  - squad number, squad status and listing;
  - a swap through the dialog.
- **Squads:**
  - Manchester United's captains, and their swap;
  - heal all (7), inspire all (28) and refresh all (36);
  - the Squad tab's Inspire all and its Undo.
- **The language switch and the graphics packs.**

This save plays women's football too. Of the 52 clubs of the 200 best players, 18
have a logo in the test pack; the women's clubs have none, and show their initials.
